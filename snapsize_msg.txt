endpoint: a filesystem snapshot reports its size when taken, not its source's now

CSI size_bytes is the snapshot's size, the minimum a restore needs, and
CreateSnapshot retries must answer the same. A filesystem snapshot has no
refquota, so snapshotView reported the source's current refquota: after
an expand, ListSnapshots and a CreateSnapshot retry reported the grown
size, and a restore from the snapshot started at it.

CreateSnapshot now records <prefix>:snapshot_size (the source's refquota,
0 for unlimited) in the same `zfs snapshot -o` as the markers, so it adds
no crash point. The scan reads it when set locally; snapshotView and the
restore's sourceSize prefer it. Zvol snapshots record nothing: they keep
the volsize they were cut at (SNP-11), which both already read, as does
the clone headroom guard. Snapshots taken before this fall back to the
source's current size, as before.

TestSnapshotSizeIsItsSizeWhenTaken: create 8 MiB, snapshot, expand to
16 MiB; retry, list and restore report 8 MiB (the filesystem leg failed
before: 16 MiB). Golden rows for the recorded and legacy restore and for
fs and zvol snapshot plans.
