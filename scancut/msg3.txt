endpoint: DeleteVolume of an absent volume proves the parent by its listing

Exists (an exported pool says "does not exist" too). DeleteVolume's very
next call lists the parent's snapshots, which is Unavailable when the
parent is missing, so that check was a second proof of the same thing.

not-found as the unproven cause, and scan proves it as before
(proveParent, which keeps that cause in the Unavailable error).
scanDelete uses scanUnproven. The order is the old one: the volume's
scan, then the parent's proof, both under vol:<id>.
