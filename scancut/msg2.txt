endpoint: no re-read when a create or expand applied nothing

A compatible CreateVolume repeat and an ExpandVolume already at its
capacity apply no op, yet re-read the volume they had just scanned under
the same lock: the same rows twice. servedFrom answers from that scan
when its row is a served volume, and falls back to the re-read (and its
verdict) otherwise. CreateSnapshot already re-reads only after an op.

Saves one scan per repeat: block, filesystem and NFS retries 2 -> 1,
clone retries 3 -> 2, expand retry 2 -> 1 (TestRPCCallBudget updated).
Endpoint calls over the simulator suite: 12392 -> 11998.
