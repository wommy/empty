endpoint: guard the scan paths before cutting calls

Three tests, all passing on the current code, that pin what the planned
scan cuts (wave24 scan-reduction P1, P2/P3, P6) could break:

- TestMissingParentFailsCreateAndDeleteUnavailable: with the parent
  destroyed, CreateVolume (block, filesystem, clone of a snapshot, clone
  of a volume, an existing name) and DeleteVolume (never made, block,
  filesystem) are each Unavailable, never NotFound and never a create.
  The prototype's first parent cut made a block create NotFound while
  the suite stayed green.
- TestCreateVolumeCatchesDatasetReplacedBeforeCommit: a dataset renamed
  away and replaced by an unowned one just before the commit `zfs set`
  is caught by the re-read (Aborted), neither adopted nor destroyed.
  Nothing guarded this. The filesystem case also runs on a real pool.
- TestRPCCallBudget: each main RPC path's zfs call sequence on the
  simulator (new and retried block, filesystem, NFS and clone creates,
  snapshot, expand, delete present and absent), recorded by the new
  RecordCalls next to CountCalls. A mismatch prints the replacement
  table. Simulator only.

Weights for the three in ci/real-zfs-weights.txt.
