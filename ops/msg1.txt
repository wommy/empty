reconcile: summary and undesired lines, -fail-on-findings (exit 3) in the timer

The daily report-only reconcile exited 0 whatever it found, as plain text
nobody read. The report now ends with

  undesired: N (max M[: apply would be refused])
  summary: uncommitted=N undesired=N orphan_clone_sources=N exposures=N findings=N skipped=N skipped_<kind>=N ...

and -fail-on-findings exits 3 when the summary counts a finding (an
action, or a skip other than young, busy, changed, empty-desired-set or
not-ours). The reconcile timer's unit passes it, so a report with findings
fails the unit and shows in systemctl --failed.

Reconcile also reports an abandoned create still exported by its own
sharenfs (SkipUncommittedExported, counted as an exposure with
below-floor networks), and the endpoint logs one JSON "reconcile result"
line with the same counts per call (endpoint.ReconcileSummary).
