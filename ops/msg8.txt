README: "Runbook: when a PVC is stuck", one paragraph per error family

Where to look (journalctl -u 'zfs-endpoint@*' -p warning; grep the
volume id; follow a request_id), then one short paragraph each for
ABORTED (lock wait with its holder, after a change, out of space after a
change), RESOURCE_EXHAUSTED, UNAVAILABLE, FAILED_PRECONDITION (NFS
exposure, dependent clone, not managed, snapshots) and INTERNAL.

deploy.TestRunbookQuotesLiveMessages pins every message the runbook
quotes to the endpoint's non-test sources, so a reworded message fails
the build instead of leaving the runbook pointing at nothing.
