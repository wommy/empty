create. The health probe's existing `zfs get` of the parent now reads
`available` with `type` (no extra fork), and with -min-free-bytes set it
logs a WARN "health: free space below -min-free-bytes" once when the
space falls below the floor and an INFO once it is back. Health stays
SERVING, like the floor it reports on.

An out-of-space failure after a change stays ABORTED (RESOURCE_EXHAUSTED
would strand the half-made create), but its message no longer promises
plain convergence: "(earlier ops applied; out of space: free space on
the pool first, then a retry converges)", and likewise for a resumed
attempt.
