zfs: "zfs slow" warning for any command, reads included, past -zfs-slow (10s)

A zfs hung on a suspended pool left no line: reads are not audited and
the rpc line is logged only when the call returns. zfs.AuditSlow logs one
WARN "zfs slow" line, while the command still runs, once it passes the
threshold: argv[0..2], the target (last argument), elapsed, and the
call's request_id. zfs.Audit keeps its signature with DefaultSlow (10s);
the endpoint's -zfs-slow sets it (0: off, negative refused, 78). The
clock starts once the command has a -max-zfs slot.
