endpoint: model-based suite with expiring masks, and real-zfs facts

TestModel runs random sequences of RPCs and out-of-band actions against
the endpoint and a pure reference model (model_test.go), injecting kills,
errors and races at plan steps, and shrinks a failure to a replayable
seed. Known divergences are masked with a spec row and an expiry
(D07/VOL-06, GAP-RECRACE/OWN-15, both 2026-12-15); an expired mask fails
TestModel. The OWN-12 and HOLD-REPLAN masks are gone: the planner now
refuses held snapshots and datasets below a volume, and the model agrees.

Budget (ADR 0005): a PR runs 64 sequences on the simulator (about 3 s
under -race; the endpoint package stays near 9 s) and MODEL_BUDGET=50
steps on a real pool. The nightly tier (model_nightly_test.go) sweeps
20,000 sequences from a daily seed (4m13s measured) and checks that each
mask still finds a failure.

TestRealFacts checks the OpenZFS facts that the model and the simulator
share against the real pool. CR-03 now states that lzc_destroy_snaps is
atomic within one batch. SNP-11 no longer expects the simulator to
diverge.

Mutants at PR size: TestModel kills the name guard, received-as-local and
unstamped-clone-source mutants. The held-snapshot and child-dataset
planner mutants are killed at 2,000 sequences (nightly) and by planner
tests on PR. Clone-source-uses-OwnedVolume survives the model, because
the generator does not make uncommitted volumes; TestCloneSourcesMustBeOwned
kills it.
