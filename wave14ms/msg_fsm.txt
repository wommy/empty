endpoint: recovery FSM explorer runs nightly, and the guard that it ran

The wave5 lifecycle explorer comes in as TestExploreLifecycle under
`//go:build nightly`: breadth-first to depth 4 over both volume kinds and
both property prefixes, with crashes at every mutation, deadline kills,
partial commands and out-of-band races, about 40 s on 4 cores. It now
fails on any finding instead of only writing a report.

Its classifier moves to fsm_classify_test.go (untagged, pure) with a row
table test, TestClassify, and two fixes:
- AN-09: the clone source CreateVolume-from-volume stamps (managed +
  clone_source_for, Row.OwnedCloneSource) is a legal state,
  OwnCloneSrc-live/orphan/deferred, not IMPOSSIBLE.
- a deferred snapshot kept by a user hold, without clones, is legal.

The explorer also gains a user hold and a dataset below a volume as
out-of-band actions, a reconcile with a proper subset of the committed
volumes as desired, "failed" groups in the reconcile response (a group
that fails on every run made no action, so it read as idempotent), and a
raw `zfs get userrefs` in each world's identity, so a scan that drops
children or holds cannot merge worlds zfs tells apart.

ci/check-explore-ran.sh is wired after the explore step in nightly.yml:
a run in which no TestScale*/TestExplore* test passed fails. Locally:
with -tags nightly 1 test passed and the guard exits 0; without the tag
the same command is "[no tests to run]" and the guard exits 1.

- classify AN-09 fix reverted: TestClassify, TestExploreLifecycle
- scan ignores userrefs: TestExploreLifecycle
- scan or planner ignores children: TestExploreLifecycle
- planner ignores holds: survives the explorer (no path to an undesired
