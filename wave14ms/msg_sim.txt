zfstest: model user holds, rename, guid, and no datasets below a zvol

The simulator gains what an operator does out of band on a real pool:
`zfs hold`/`release` (userrefs; a plain destroy of a held snapshot is
EBUSY with libzfs's "it's being held" text, -d marks it, the last
release reaps it, and a held deferred origin outlives its last clone),
`zfs rename` with guids that follow the dataset, and zfs's "parent is
not a filesystem" for create or clone below a zvol. The new commands
live in sim_ext.go; sim.go only gains the hooks.

FuzzReconcileSafe made a child dataset below a clone of a zvol, which
real zfs refuses; it now tracks zvol clones (seed 9abef424 kept).
