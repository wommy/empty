## Key finding: the ssh poll, not the guest, is the boot's tail
wait_ssh uses ConnectTimeout=5. slirp's hostfwd accepts the TCP connect at once; while the guest
has no address the SYN to 10.0.2.15 is retransmitted by slirp (BSD RTO ~3s) and ssh waits for the
banner up to ConnectTimeout (OpenSSH applies it to the identification exchange). Endpoint: ssh.service
active ~+6.2, observed +9.2 (gap ~3s). csi: gap ~6s (> one 5s timeout). Fix: per-attempt
ConnectTimeout=1 / `timeout 1`, overlapping probes, or gate on console.log "Started ssh".
