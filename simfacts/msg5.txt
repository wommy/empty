realfacts: VOL-12, copies=2 does not grow a thick zvol's reservation

zvol_volsize_to_reservation reads copies with nvlist_lookup_string, but
zfs create passes real_props, where zfs_valid_proplist has already made
the index property a uint64, so the lookup fails and ncopies stays 1;
the set paths get validated lists too. The Sim's autoResv ignores copies
and is right. The row pins that on a real pool by comparing the two
refreservations, not a number, so it holds on any vdev layout.

The Sim passes it before and after this series (nothing in the Sim
changes); a Sim whose create path multiplies the auto reservation by
copies fails it (3145728 against 6291456).
