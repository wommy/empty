zfstest, zfsdiff: available and the space checks as dsl_dir computes them

dd_used_bytes charges each child dir the larger of its used and its
reservation, not their sum, so a refreservation inside a reservation is
counted once (dsl_dir_diduse_transfer_space). available is
dsl_dir_space_available (the parent's space plus the dir's own unused
reservation, capped by its quota) plus the head's unused refreservation,
capped by refquota (dsl_dataset_space). The refreservation check now
measures growth against the dir's space (no add-back, no refquota cap),
and the reservation check the MAX(used, new) - MAX(used, old) delta
against the parent's space. Referenced and unique stay 0.

The zfsdiff model had the same sum and changes with the Sim, or
TestCorpusModelVsSim splits on the two new corpus sequences
(resv-grow-into-parent-resv, quota-over-child-resv-and-refresv; full
corpus only, not PRSample). TestRealFacts SPC-01 pins available on a
real pool: at the parent of this commit the Sim reports 192 MiB where a
reservation should leave 256 MiB, and 1 TiB under a 16 MiB refquota.

The endpoint is unchanged. Its headroom guard reads available on the
parent, which on a real pool includes the parent's own unused
refreservation and is capped by its refquota; the Sim now shows both.
