zfstest: a mountpoint change refused busy remounts and reshares

changelist_prefix walks the list deepest mountpoint first. When
zfs_unmount fails on a node (a pinned child is mounted below it), it
shares that node again and commits, every later node loses its
needpost, and changelist_postfix runs over the nodes before it, which
remounts and reshares the inherited children it had unmounted. The Sim
returned busy and left them unmounted and out of zfs.exports.

TestRealFacts MNT-02 moves a parent over a pinned child and checks that
the move fails busy and the inherited child is mounted again; it fails on
the Sim before this commit (mounted=no).
