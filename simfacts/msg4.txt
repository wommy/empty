zfstest: canmount=off unmounts through a mountpoint changelist

zfs_prop_set_list_flags gathers canmount=off on a mounted filesystem
(only then), and changelist_gather maps canmount to a mountpoint list:
prefix unmounts the filesystem and the children inheriting its
mountpoint, and fails busy over a pinned child mounted inside it, before
canmount is set. postfix remounts the children (cn_mounted); the
filesystem itself is no longer mountable. The Sim unmounted only the
filesystem, and never failed.

TestRealFacts MNT-01 sets canmount=off over a pinned child and wants
busy with the parent still mounted and canmount=on; it fails on the Sim
before this commit (the set succeeds).
