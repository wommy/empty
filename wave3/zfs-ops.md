# ZFS operational edges: zfs-endpoint

Scope: how the `zfs` CLI behaves in the edges below, and whether `internal/zfs` and `internal/endpoint` handle them.

## Provenance and caveats

- **OpenZFS source used**: `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-empty/d2383434-1d2e-5ac5-931d-d25ccb26a882/scratchpad/repos/openzfs`. Its `META` says **2.4.99 (dev)** and the checkout has no tags. It is not 2.4.4 and not 2.2. All source references are to this snapshot. Message text and behaviour may differ in the 2.4.4 and 2.2 releases and must be checked against those tags.
- **Not available in this container**: `zfs`/`zpool` binaries and the ZFS kernel module. Anything marked UNVERIFIED is a source reading, not a runtime observation.
- **Our code** (`/home/user/empty`) was read only. Line numbers refer to the current tree.
- **Scale measurement** (`scratchpad/wave3/bench/zfs/scale_test.go`): a synthetic `zfs get -Hp -r` stream in the real shape (27 properties per dataset, the list in `internal/endpoint/table.go` `columns()`, 10k volumes plus 100k snapshots) fed to the real `ListColumns`/`parseColumns` with a fake runner. This measures our parser, not `zfs`.
- **Injection proof** (`scratchpad/wave3/bench/zfs/inject_test.go`): runs the real `parseColumns` on zfs-shaped output that contains a forged line. It passes and shows the forged ownership.
- **Kernel version**: `ZFS_ERR_IOC_CMD_UNAVAIL`, `zoned_uid` and the `zfs get -j` JSON code are in this 2.4.99 snapshot. Their presence in 2.4.4 and 2.2 is UNVERIFIED.
