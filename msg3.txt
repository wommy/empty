endpoint: CreateSnapshot after the cut is never final

Once `zfs snapshot` succeeded, CreateSnapshot's re-read failing
unclassified was Internal, and a snapshot gone by the re-read was
Internal "vanished after creation". Both are final for
external-snapshotter v8.2.0, which then drops the being-created
annotation: the VolumeSnapshotContent can be deleted without a
DeleteSnapshot, leaking the snapshot, and its source's DeleteVolume is
then refused for good ("has snapshot").

The re-read's failure now goes through afterChange (resumeErr's mapping,
with its own reason), and the vanished case is Aborted. Kept local to
the error mapping: the snapshot size path is untouched.

TestCreateSnapshotAfterCutIsRetryable: both cases were code 13, are
Aborted now, and a retry returns the same id with one snapshot listed.
