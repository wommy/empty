zfstest: space checks read received space properties

num() and spaceCheck's get() read only the local value, so a received
refreservation (a thick zvol from `zfs send -p | zfs recv`) charged
nothing: the sim accepted a quota below it and a second thick volume
over the parent's quota, both of which OpenZFS refuses with ENOSPC (the
kernel reads the effective value: local, else received). Read the
effective value through node.effective, the rule the property printer
already applies.
