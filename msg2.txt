endpoint: a read that fails after CreateVolume's change is Aborted

The error table's after-change Aborted row covered only the apply ops, so
remapMutated could not re-key a scan or get, and CreateVolume returned its
reads after a change raw anyway: the second pass's scan, the headroom
read, verifyExport's scan and the re-read after the commit. An
unclassified failure there (unknown stderr, an undecodable scan) was
Internal, final for external-provisioner, stranding the half-made (for
NFS, exported) volume once the PVC is deleted, or a committed volume no
PV ever names.

- errors.go: the row drops its ops restriction, so KindUnknown after a
  change is Aborted for every op, reads included.
- CreateVolume marks the volume changed once a pass applied an op or the
  scan found it uncommitted, and sends every later failure through
  resumeErr, the re-read after the commit included.
- resumeErr also takes a cell-less Internal (a scan whose output does not
  decode) as unclassified: Aborted. Every other code is a refusal made on
  purpose and is kept: FailedPrecondition "served beyond its export",
  NotFound, Unavailable stay as they were.

TestCreateVolumeReadAfterChangeIsRetryable fails the second-pass scan,
verifyExport's scan and the post-commit re-read (plain and NFS): code 13
before, Aborted now, and a retry commits. TestResumeErrKeepsRefusals pins
the refusals; TestCreateVolumeReadBeforeChangeStaysInternal pins Internal
before any change. TestErrTableMatchesHead lists the changed cells
(changedSinceHead).

docs: sidecar-conformance.md said non-gRPC errors are final in the
provisioner; v5.2.0's checkError returns InBackground for them. README's
ABORTED line now states the wider rule.
